Funspot — Live Football Fan Arena

Funspot is a live football fan engagement app. Join channel-based voting on real fixtures, chat live with other fans during matches, and climb the leaderboard every season.

What you can do on Funspot

  • Arena — vote on upcoming and live fixtures, join channels with other fans, and chat during matches.
  • Feed — share photos and video with the community and follow other fans' posts.
  • History — browse settled fixtures, past results, and channel leaderboards from previous seasons.

Funspot channels are built around real football fixtures across major leagues, with live commentary and sub-fixture markets (first to score, first corner, first yellow card, and more) that fans can vote and chat about together.
